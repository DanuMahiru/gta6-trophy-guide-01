    Platinum roadmap

    One playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay, about 200 hours. Cheats disable trophies and saving — do not use them.

    Missables & at risk

    Only one trophy is possibly missable. Five more are at risk because their quest chains are optional and go quiet for chapters. Five trophies still have no confirmed unlock method — those are listed honestly as unconfirmed, not guessed.
